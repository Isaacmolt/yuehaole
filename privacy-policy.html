資料收集

約好了不會收集、傳輸或分享您的任何個人資料。所有客人資料、預約紀錄與作品照片僅儲存在您的裝置本機。

照片與相機

只有在您主動選擇或拍攝作品照時，App 才會存取相簿或相機。照片只儲存在裝置內，不會上傳到任何伺服器。

通知

預約提醒與回訪提醒全部由裝置本機排程，不經過任何伺服器。

備份

您可以自行匯出備份檔案並透過您選擇的方式保存。開發者無法存取這些檔案。

第三方服務

約好了不使用任何第三方分析工具、廣告 SDK 或追蹤技術。購買功能由 Apple App Store 處理。

兒童隱私

約好了並非針對 13 歲以下兒童設計，我們不會有意收集兒童的個人資料。

政策變更

若本隱私權政策有所變更，我們會在此頁面更新。

Booked (約好了) Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 5, 2026

Data collection

Booked does not collect, transmit or share any personal data. All client records, bookings and photos are stored only on your device.

Photos and camera

The app accesses your photo library or camera only when you choose to add a photo. Photos stay on your device and are never uploaded.

Notifications

Booking and due-back reminders are scheduled locally on your device. No server is involved.

Backups

You can export a backup file and keep it wherever you choose. The developer has no access to these files.

Third-party services

Booked uses no analytics, advertising SDKs or tracking. Purchases are handled by the Apple App Store.

Children's privacy

Booked is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.
